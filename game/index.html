Wod dem · Words

Make sentences by pushing word tiles into a line, left-to-right or top-to-bottom: THING NA PROPERTY (e.g. BOBO NA MI = "boy is you"), or THING NA THING to change one thing into another (e.g. ROK NA FLAG). Use EN ("and") to join more words. Bright tiles are part of a rule.
